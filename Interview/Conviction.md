# What is an interview?

Wrong answer: a conversation. Go to a call with somebody at a company and respond his questions
Correct answer: a sales process. You're the product

## Exchange of value

Company (buyer, $) -> You (product, time, energy, exp)

## Your job in the interview

Convince the interviewer that the exchange is going to benefit the company

## What happens in an interview?

Not only matters what you say, also how you say it.

Rational (resume, qualifications, stats)
Irrational (emotional, confidence, conviction, how the "feel" after)

## Why conviction matters?

If someone buys a product, it is because they believe that the value for that product is greater for the cost they pay to purchase it.

## Transfer of conviction

Sales is convincing someone to buy something is simply is a transfer of your convinction and your belief in this product to them.
You have to believe you are the right hire. You have to believe strongly enought that they feel this.

The interviewer perspective: the're interviewing 10-100 of candidates, if they hire the wrong person, it not going to be productive, helful, etc. It is a risk, so they're coming in the interiew with doubt and scepticism. We need to come with enought conviction so they choose to trust our conviction over they doubts.

## Belief in your words

It doesn't matters what you tell, but how you tell it. You have believe in your words, if they can sense that you're believing in yourself.

## How to build conviction?

- Have a killer product (you)
- Reverse engineer (goals -> plan, connect the doots)
- Stack evidence
- Do the hard work even when you don't feel like it
