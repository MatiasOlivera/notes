# Success rate

There is only one question going into your interviewer's mind:
~~What do you bring to the table?~~

**Do I want this person on my team?**

If you pass the HR screen and application process and make it to the interview, that already means you have the basic qualifications they're looking for.

## Why is passing an interview not just about qualifications?

99% of people do not make it past the application, especially in competitive positions. There are thousands of applications for one position.

- ~~Qualifications/stats~~

- Impressive, but not arrogant
  
  - Would I want this person sitting next to me every single day?
    
    Would I be able to stand their personality?
    
    It has a lot more to do with them liking you as a person than the achievements that you have.

- Personable, but professional
  
  - Avoid being too casual
  
  - You're talking to a stranger
  
  - They are trying to evaluate you, not just having a conversation

- Someone who won't embarrass them!
  
  - If a person passes you in an interview, it means you have the stamp of approval. If they made a mistake, the other person would ask, "Do you think this person was the right candidate?"

## Actionables

- impressive: the hard stats, experience. When you talk about yourself, you're humble and confident
  
  - Confidence: state the facts, being comfortable with who you are
  
  - Arrogance: You are better than someone because of your accomplishments

- Personable
  
  - Conveying warmth, a smile. You should consider a win if you get the interviewer's smile as well. If they smile, they feel good and associate these feelings with you.
  
  - Don't share too much! Read the room, adjust your tonality and language to the way their speaking. Adjust the way that you talk and your energy level according to the interviewer

- Won't embarrass them

## Why the stakes are so high?

The cost of acquiring a new employee is high for most companies. They're probably going to be negative at the beginning because your productivity is 0 or negative when you're just learning and starting at the job. You're going to be positive 6 months or more into the job. They are taking a big bet on you by hiring you.
