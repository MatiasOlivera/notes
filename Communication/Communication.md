## Self-awareness

Record and review: the only way to become more aware is if you see yourself. 

1. Record a video of you speaking for 5 minutes

2. Review and reflect on the video

3. Mute the video, look at yourself, and take notes

4. Turn the volume up, listen to yourself. What do you like? What don't you like?

5. Transcribe

## Articulate

To improve articulation and pronunciation: grab a book, 4x5 min a day, read the book out loud, overdue your lips/tongue movements, and record/review. Next level: put a pen in your mouth, you're forced to overarticulate

## Pause

If you think the pause is useless, you start with the behavior of filling the pause. The pause allows people to process what you are saying
