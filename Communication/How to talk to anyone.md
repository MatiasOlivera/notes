# How to talk to anyone

## 4 types of communicators

Red: power and results

Green: peace and stability

Blue: logic and structure

Yellow: fun and connection

Why does talking to some people feel effortless, and talking to others feel impossible? You need to learn how to speak their language, so you can connect with them

> Surrounded by idiots - Thomas Erikson

## Colors

### Red

Bold, decisive, comparative, result-driven, impasient

**Impact:**

- Green: feels you're pushy and overwhelming

- Yellow: sees you as too serious and blunt

- Blue: thinks you're reckless with details

### Green

Calm, supportive, reliable, don't like sudden changes, extremely passionate

**Impact:**

- Red: thinks you're indecisive and too slow to act

- Yellow: finds you boring or resistant to fun

- Blue: gets frustrated when you avoid details or decisions

### Blue

Precise, detail-oriented, cautious, critical thinkers, perfectionists

**Impact:**

- Red: loses patience and sees you too slow

- Yellow: feels judged by your criticism

- Green: thinks you're nitpicky and disrupting the peace

### Yellow

Social, enthusiastic, likes to talk, creative, optimistic

**Impact:**

- Red: find you unfocused and impatient with your tangents

- Green: feels your energy is chaotic and draining

- Blue: sees you as superficial and unreliable

### Identifying your color

Are you introverted (blue/green) or extroverted (red/yellow)?

Am I more logical (green/red) or emotional (blue/yellow)?

Do I speak deliberately (green/blue) or quickly (yellow/red)?

## The problem with each color

Most people communicate in their own color, assuming everybody around them communicates the same way. We tend to default to our communication style, and it's great when you are speaking to people just like you.

Mastering this isn't about you knowing your color, it's about learning to speak all of the colors.

When you only speak your own color, you only connect with people who are like you and the same color as you.

Do not change the essence of who you are. If you want to be a more effective communicator, you need to learn how to adjust your delivery and speak in their communication style so you can connect with them

### Green

You can connect with everyone. That doesn't mean that you're changing who you are; it means you're learning how to adapt your communication style to them. Masterful communicator meets people where they are and makes the connection efortless
