# Speaking mistake

When you pair technical skills with great communication skills, that's when you thrive.

If you're 10/10 technically but 3/10 communicatively, do you think people perceive you that way? We may raise our communication level, and the world perceives our communication + technical skills.

Learn improvisation.

## The storytelling formula

The way most people tell stories is that they report stories.

VAKS storytelling: visual, auditory, kinesthetic, smell.

Hook, struggle, breakthrough, application.

## Clutter words

Replace clutter with pause.
