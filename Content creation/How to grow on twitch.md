### Focus on one audience
Plan your content with an audience in mind. An audience gravitates around the same topics because they like it.

### Not everything you love is content
Not everything in your life is meant to be content. Pick n audience, appeal to them, keep the rest for yourself.

### You need a schedule
Pick the days and hours an go live.

### Fix your stream with the 15 rule
Go to your latest stream and watch the first 15 minute. If so boring you can't watch it, you need to fix your content. Make notes on what to improve. Skip ahead and watch for 5 minutes to simulate a new viewer

### You need to care about your stream title
Write better titles. Write something funny or an opinion. Write something that gets a click!

### It's not just gaming
Plan ideas, concepts, challenges to overcome. A viewer should understand the goal from the title

### Why episodic content doesn't work anymore
If you create content that require previous knowledge. If you do that, that content will likely fail. Publish one large edit that any viewer can enjoy without doing to do homework

### Why you don't need a discord
The time investment to keep it active does not worth it

### How to get more clicks?

### How to boost your short form retention?
If the first seconds aren't understandable or relatable you are gonna keep swapping. You want your first seconds to be relatable so they don't swipe away

Stream clips converted into short format will fail the testing phase. Stream clips almost always fails because they don't have a format.
