## Market analysis

## Choose your value prop
How you can stand out? How your value is singular? What people are talking about in similar videos? First 7/30 seconds, how people open videos? Which are the popular formats: versus, challenges? Why your audience is watching your videos?

## Write 100 video titles

## Find a testing ground
Put your idea into the world and see if it resonates. Get feedback. Test in short form, reuse the idea in long form.

## Film. Edit. Upload. Repeat

### Scripting
- Write the video BEFORE you film. Set title, thumbnail, hook, beginning, middle, end.
- Does the hook meets the expectations of the viewer?
### Start with shorts
- Upload a short and long form video about the same topic

### Which data matters
- click ratio: how well your ideas are resonating? are you putting enough curiosity into the viewer? how many people that view the thumbnail click it? 10% is great
- average view duration
- how to know if your growing? new vs returning viewers
